# DualDock 2.8.6

Dock 设置侧栏将“屏幕与位置”和“显示内容”合并为 **多屏和显示内容**。右侧仍可分别设置各屏幕的位置和 Dock 显示的应用。搜索原名称会定位到对应分区。

## English

The Dock settings sidebar now has one **Displays & Content** entry. Display placement and the apps shown in the Dock keep their own sections. Searching for either original name takes you to that section.
