# DualDock 2.8.2

- 设置窗口默认 820 × 560 pt。切换分类、切换语言或展开高级设置时保持原位。正文和选项字号放大，交互和通用设置按主题连续排列，可从侧栏跳到对应位置。
- 活动应用的右键菜单改为“固定到 DualDock”。固定时可看到图标移入固定区，拖入应用夹时图标会缩进文件夹。打开应用夹时有轻微淡入。
- 应用启动时，图标朝屏幕内侧轻轻抬起再落下。应用退出时，活动区图标淡出，已固定的图标留在原位。开启 macOS 的“减少动态效果”后直接显示操作结果。

## English

- Settings opens at 820 × 560 pt. The window stays in place as you switch sections or languages and expand advanced options. Larger text and continuous forms make related settings easier to read together.
- Choose **Pin to DualDock** to move a running app into the pinned area. Dropping an app into a group shows its icon settling into the folder. Groups fade in when opened.
- Launching an app gently lifts its icon. Quitting fades running-only icons out and keeps pinned icons in place. The transitions respect the macOS Reduce Motion setting.
