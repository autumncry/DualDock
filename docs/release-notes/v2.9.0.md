# DualDock 2.9.0

### 应用组外观

在 **设置 → Dock → 应用组外观** 中为所有屏幕的应用组选择图标。

- **银灰**沿用四格应用托盘，**经典**还原旧版蓝色文件夹和叠放的成员图标。新加入的三款分别是**扁平、皮革和玻璃**。
- 每款风格都支持自定义背景色与 0–100% 背景不透明度。0% 时只隐藏文件夹底板，成员图标、运行状态和消息提醒仍可见。
- 升级后默认使用银灰风格。可随时恢复风格原色或默认外观。

### 自动隐藏 Dock

在 **设置 → 交互 → 窗口与预览** 开启 **自动隐藏 Dock** 后，指针停在某块屏幕的 Dock 边缘即可唤出，离开后自动收起。使用菜单、分组、预览或拖拽时保持显示。该选项默认关闭。全屏应用仍然独立支持边缘唤出。

## English

Open **Settings → Dock → Group Appearance** to choose a style for app groups across your displays. Silver keeps the four-icon tray, Classic brings back the blue folder, and Flat, Leather and Glass offer three new looks. Set a custom background color and adjust background opacity from 0 to 100%. At 0%, member icons, running indicators and notification badges remain visible.

In **Settings → Interaction → Windows & Previews**, enable **Automatically hide the Dock** to reveal DualDock at the configured edge and hide it after the pointer leaves. It stays open while you use a group, menu or preview, or drag an item. The option is off by default. Full-screen Spaces continue to support edge reveal independently.
